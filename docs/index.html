ElukkaSynth

A free additive and resynthesis synthesizer. Build sounds from hundreds of partials, resynthesize audio files and images, and shape them with unison, tuning, a step sequencer and reverb. VST3 instrument for macOS, Windows and Linux.

Download for macOS Universal (Apple Silicon + Intel), macOS 11+ Download for Windows 64-bit, Windows 10 / 11 Download for Linux x86-64, glibc 2.35+ (Ubuntu 22.04 or newer)
